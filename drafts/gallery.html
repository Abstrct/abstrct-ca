Abstrct: Games Inside Things

A retrospective. Josh McDougall’s practice since 2010 places games inside systems that were never meant to hold them. Please touch the art. That is the art.

1

Josh McDougall (Abstrct)

Schemaverse, 2011

PostgreSQL, PL/pgSQL, 108 players

First shown at DEF CON 19

The artist placed a space war inside a relational database and invited strangers to attack it. Over four days they ran more than a million queries. The database survived. The strangers report mixed results.

2

Josh McDougall (Abstrct)

Coindroids, 2014–2020

DEFCOIN, robots, money

Shown at DEF CON 22 through 28

Robots shoot money at each other. At DEF CON 26 the work accounted for more than half of all DEFCOIN transactions, making it one of the few artworks that is also an economy.

3

Josh McDougall (Abstrct), with Slow Ninja

Structs, ongoing

Its own blockchain, Alpha Matter, humans, AI agents

Collection of the players

A space strategy game that runs on its own chain. Several of its players are not people. The artist considers this a feature.

Josh McDougall (Abstrct)

Four Studies of Structs, 2026

Toy box (pippop), dragons (wyrmhodl), Dreamcast (structs.xyz), fur (Furrency)

The same game, painted four more times. Compare the artist’s other series, “The Same Game, Painted Again.”

8

Josh McDougall (Abstrct)

Toronto Games, 2026

65816 assembly on Super Nintendo, 512 KB

Seven events across Toronto, including Bike Courier, Off-Leash Alert and TTC Surfing. Visitors are encouraged to attempt Parallel Parking in the Annex and to manage their expectations.

9

Josh McDougall (Abstrct), with others

Untitled (Standard), 2015

The CryptoCurrency Security Standard

Collection of C4, co-founded by the artist, 2014

A rare work in which the artist writes the rules rather than playing inside them. Critics remain divided over whether this is irony.
