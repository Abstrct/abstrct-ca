Before we get to the recipe, a little about me.

I first made this dish in 2011, in a PostgreSQL database, for 108 people at DEF CON. They ran over a million queries in four days. Some of them still write in asking when I’ll make it again.

Since then I’ve made it with a cryptocurrency, an entire blockchain and a Super Nintendo. I’ve made it at conferences that had already started. I co-founded a non-profit and helped write a security standard, which is sort of like reading the label on the jar for once.

Games Inside Things

PrepOne conference
CookSince 2010
Serves108 (DEF CON 19)

Ingredients

  • 1 PostgreSQL database, room temperature
  • A generous pinch of PL/pgSQL
  • DEFCOIN, to taste
  • 1 blockchain, built on the Cosmos SDK, with Alpha Matter
  • 1 Super Nintendo, plus 512 KB of 65816 assembly
  • 1 studio (Slow Ninja)
  • Strangers, to serve

Method

  1. Take the database. Put a space war in it. Invite DEF CON to attack it. Serve as Schemaverse.
  2. Replace the database with a cryptocurrency. Teach robots to shoot money at each other. Serve as Coindroids, at DEF CON 22 through 28.
  3. Scale up to a whole blockchain. Allow AI agents at the table. Serve as Structs.
  4. Plate the same dish four more ways: pippop, wyrmhodl, structs.xyz, Furrency.
  5. Cool slightly. Pour into a Super Nintendo. Garnish with TTC Surfing. Serve as Toronto Games.
  6. If a conference has already started, make something useful in a hurry (Tokens.fyi, Key Ceremony Guide).
Chef’s notes. Pairs well with a DJ set. If you don’t have a database, a blockchain works. If you don’t have a blockchain, honestly, most things work. For food safety, see C4. More recipes at github.com/abstrct and @abstr_ct.
